Set up your service desk

Create an editable service desk group and a laptop offering routed to that group.

Start taking work

  1. Create or invite your technicians and requesters. Use the agent role for technicians.
  2. Add technicians to the incident team.
  3. Edit the starter laptop offering and its fulfillment team.
  4. As a requester, report a problem or order a service.
  5. As a technician, open Incidents or the fulfillment queue.
