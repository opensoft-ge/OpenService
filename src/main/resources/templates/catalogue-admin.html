Manage catalogue offerings

Active offerings can be requested by eligible users with request-creation access. Eligibility is checked from current group membership. Changes affect future requests; existing requests retain their reviewed text and their own access rules.

Who can request this offering

Restrict discovery and new orders to members of one group, or allow all signed-in users. Readers still cannot order. Removing a membership or deleting the group blocks new access immediately; administrators can still inspect the offering.

Route fulfilment

New orders create one task for the selected team. Eligible staff claim it, complete the request and share an outcome with the requester. Routing does not grant access to a request. Changes affect future orders.
